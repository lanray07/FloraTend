## Short description

Organise your plant collection, plan care checks and keep dated growth notes. FloraTend keeps core plant records on your device, with no account required. Reminder dates prompt you to check conditions before deciding whether to water.

## Long description

Every plant deserves a little attention.

FloraTend brings your plant collection and care routine together. Keep a nickname, species, location and personal notes for each plant. Add your own photographs and find records by searching or filtering your collection.

Plan reminders to check soil conditions, or create fertilising, pruning, repotting and other care schedules. Record completed care, snooze a check or pause a schedule when your routine changes. A reminder is a prompt to inspect your plant, not a guarantee that it needs water.

Use the growth journal for dated notes, photographs, height measurements and leaf counts. Your observations form the record; the app does not invent growth measurements or diagnose plant health.

Core plant records, saved photographs, care schedules and journal notes are stored locally. No account is required for these core functions. Available English text commands offer another way to navigate and record care. Push-to-talk and spoken responses depend on compatible speech engines and supported languages installed on your device. Speech recognition may require a network connection.

The free collection is limited to five plants. Any available Premium purchase price is shown by Amazon for your marketplace.

FloraTend provides general organisational and educational tools. Plant needs vary by species and conditions. For persistent or severe plant problems, seek appropriate horticultural advice.

## Feature bullets

- Plan care checks and record your completed care history.
- Save dated growth notes, height measurements and leaf observations.
- Access local core records without a required account.
- Use English text commands; optional speech depends on device support.
