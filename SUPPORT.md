# FloraTend support

For help with FloraTend, email **banksmi@mail.com**.

You can also report a bug or suggest an improvement in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/lanray07/FloraTend/issues). Issues are public. Please keep personal records, photographs, Amazon receipts and account details out of public reports.

Include your FloraTend version, Fire tablet/Android model, operating-system version and the steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot is useful when it contains no private information.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account or an internet connection?** Core plant records, care schedules and journal notes are stored on your device and require no separate FloraTend account. Optional Amazon purchasing and installed speech services may require a connection.

**Why is the microphone unavailable?** Some Fire tablets have no compatible Android speech-recognition service. Use the touch controls or type an assistant command. Spoken feedback also depends on an installed voice that supports the selected language.

**Are translations reviewed?** Additional UI translations and command dictionaries are currently previews. Human language review and further device verification remain pending.

**Does a watering reminder mean I should water?** No. Check the plant, soil moisture and drainage before deciding. Conditions and species differ.

**Is Premium available?** Purchasing depends on the configured Amazon product and marketplace availability. Use the app's store-supplied price and restore controls when available. Do not share your Amazon account credentials with support.

Read the [privacy notice](https://github.com/lanray07/FloraTend/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) for local storage, speech-provider processing, purchases, export and deletion details.
